Help us fix it faster

A good report includes:

  • The product version and your Windows version.
  • What happened, what you expected, and the steps that lead to it.
  • Any error text or log lines. For Syslog Manager, check the Windows Event Log (Application log, source “VSoftSol Syslog Manager”).
